# Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** School leadership and the designated privacy/security officer  
**Applies to:** Employees, contractors, students, parents/guardians, and other authorized CampusOS users  
**Review:** At least annually and after a material security or legal change

## 1. Purpose and conditions of access

CampusOS is provided only for authorized educational, administrative, safeguarding, and financial activities. Each user must use their own named account, follow school procedures, and access only information needed for their role. Access is a privilege and may be limited, reviewed, suspended, or removed.

Users must provide accurate account information, protect authentication factors, use a unique strong password, and report suspected disclosure or account compromise promptly. Passwords, reset links, session cookies, and one-time codes must never be shared. Users must sign out of shared devices and keep devices updated, locked, and protected from unauthorized viewing.

## 2. Role-specific requirements

### Teachers and staff

- Access only assigned classes, students, and operational records.
- Use student information only for legitimate school duties and approved safeguarding, teaching, attendance, or administrative purposes.
- Verify the student and date before entering attendance, grades, comments, or other consequential records.
- Correct inaccurate records through the approved correction process; do not conceal changes or use another person's account.
- Do not export, photograph, print, or locally store student/guardian data unless an authorized task requires it. Protect and securely dispose of approved copies.
- Do not discuss identifiable student information in public, personal messaging channels, or with people without a verified need to know.

### Students

- View only your own records and use school-provided features for learning and school activities.
- Do not attempt to access another student's account, records, invoices, or documents; do not bypass or test access controls.
- Do not post harmful, discriminatory, threatening, unlawful, or unrelated material using the service.
- Tell a trusted school staff member about a suspected account compromise, incorrect record, or safety concern.

### Parents and guardians

- Access only records for a child to whom the school has linked and authorized your account.
- Keep your login private and notify the school promptly when custody, guardianship, contact details, or authorization changes.
- Do not share another child's information or use it for purposes outside the child's care and education.

### Administrators and privileged users

- Use privileged access only for documented job responsibilities; use a separate administrative account where available.
- Grant the least privilege needed, verify the recipient and school scope, and remove access promptly when duties end.
- Review high-risk changes, audit events, exports, and unusual access. Do not browse records out of curiosity.
- Never use production personal data for demos, development, or testing without an approved de-identification process.

## 3. Prohibited activity

Users must not:

- Share accounts, impersonate another person, or evade authentication, authorization, monitoring, or rate limits.
- Search for, copy, alter, delete, disclose, or export data without an approved role-based purpose.
- Upload malware, executable code, unauthorized scripts, or files that violate school policy or law.
- Use CampusOS to harass, discriminate, threaten, surveil, solicit, or exploit another person.
- Interfere with service availability, probe systems without written authorization, or use the system for unlawful activity.
- Enter unnecessary sensitive personal data into free-text fields or store payment-card security codes, full card numbers, or authentication secrets.

## 4. Monitoring, reporting, and enforcement

The school may log authentication and critical record changes, investigate suspected misuse, and preserve evidence consistent with law and school policy. Logs must not be used for unrelated monitoring. Users must report suspected unauthorized access, lost devices, misdirected communications, data exposure, or inaccurate high-impact records to the designated school contact promptly.

Violations may result in access restriction or suspension, school disciplinary action, contract remedies, notification to affected parties or authorities where required, and referral to law enforcement where appropriate. The school will assess incidents fairly, preserve relevant evidence, and follow applicable privacy and labor/student protections.

## 5. Acknowledgment

Before access is issued, users must receive this policy in an accessible form and acknowledge that they understand their responsibilities. The school must provide a contact for questions, correction requests, and incident reporting.
